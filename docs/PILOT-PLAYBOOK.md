# KgweboOS Botswana pilot playbook

## Pilot objective

Validate that KgweboOS helps a Botswana business move from scattered records to clearer cash decisions in the first 30 days. The pilot is not a substitute for BURS, accountant or payment-provider approval.

## Recruit 30–50 businesses in waves

Start with 10 businesses, then expand after the first two weeks:

- 6 salons/barbers/beauty businesses
- 8 small retailers and informal traders
- 6 service businesses and consultants
- 5 contractors/artisans
- 5 food, hospitality or home-based businesses

Recruit a mix of Gaborone, Francistown and one smaller/rural market where connectivity and operating conditions differ. Record industry, location type, VAT status, number of staff, current records, payment methods and whether the owner uses WhatsApp for collections.

## Onboarding protocol

1. Obtain informed pilot consent and a named business contact.
2. Explain that KgweboOS is not tax, legal, lending or accounting advice.
3. Configure business profile, VAT status, users and payment preferences.
4. Capture a baseline: current invoice process, overdue amount, time spent weekly, tools used and confidence in cash position.
5. Observe the owner creating the first customer and invoice without coaching.
6. Record the first payment and first expense.
7. Review the dashboard after seven days.
8. Conduct a 30-day interview and export check.

## Success metrics

### Activation

- Time to first invoice: target under 10 minutes
- First payment recorded: target within 7 days for businesses that receive a payment
- First expense recorded: target within 48 hours
- At least 3 active days in the first 14 days

### Value

- Owner can answer “what is safe to spend?” without help
- At least one overdue invoice receives a logged reminder
- Owner exports or shares one report
- 70% of active pilots say cash clarity improved
- Support questions decrease after the first week

### Retention

- Day 7 active: target 70%
- Day 30 active: target 50%
- Day 30: at least one invoice or payment recorded in the previous 14 days
- Premium intent: measure interest; do not pressure conversion during research

## Interview questions

1. What did you use before KgweboOS?
2. What did you fear losing or getting wrong?
3. When do you usually discover a customer has not paid?
4. What makes a payment reminder uncomfortable?
5. Which number on the dashboard did you trust most and least?
6. Did offline behavior match what you expected?
7. What would make you recommend this to another Botswana business?
8. What would make you stop using it?
9. What records does your accountant, bank or tender application ask for?
10. Which payment method should we support first for your customers?

## Partner validation checklist

### BURS / tax

- Confirm current e-invoicing/EFD rollout scope and effective dates.
- Obtain official technical documentation or an authorised-provider contact.
- Confirm required invoice fields, numbering, credit-note rules, transmission timing and archive requirements.
- Ask whether KgweboOS can integrate directly or must connect through an approved provider.
- Have a Botswana tax practitioner review VAT calculations and product wording.

### Payment providers

For each Orange Money, MyZaka, Smega, EFT and card partner, confirm:

- Merchant/API onboarding requirements
- Transaction and settlement webhooks
- Reference and reconciliation fields
- Refund/chargeback behavior
- Fees and settlement timing
- Data-processing and support contacts
- Sandbox availability

Do not describe a provider integration as live until credentials, sandbox tests, reconciliation and commercial approval are complete.

## Support taxonomy

Tag every support interaction as one of:

- onboarding
- invoice
- payment
- collections
- inventory
- expense/OCR
- offline/sync
- permissions/security
- billing
- compliance
- feature request
- bug

Review the top five tags weekly and convert repeated questions into onboarding copy, help content or product fixes.
